Teaching is inseparable from my work as a political scientist. Having taught across disciplines as varied as international relations, sociology, and political science, I bring a multidisciplinary perspective to the classroom, one that integrates both qualitative and quantitative methods. I draw on my research on political trust, inequality, and comparative politics to show students how empirical evidence helps us make sense of real political dynamics, with particular attention to Latin America. My aim is for students to leave not only with substantive knowledge, but with the analytical tools to question claims, weigh evidence, and reach their own conclusions.
